# Handover check

Work top to bottom. Every check is pass or fail; rerun a check after its fix.
A check whose opening condition does not hold passes, and its row is still
written. No check is skipped, none returns a third verdict.

1. All twelve sections of the handover contract carry content. A section that
   does not apply is named, with the reason. Section 1 records who approved
   the problem statement, or that approval was not sought. A handover with no
   section 12 fails here.
   Fix: write the missing section.

2. The criteria set is marked frozen, the freeze is dated before the option set
   was written, and the frozen set has the same members now that it had at the
   freeze. Compare the two lists item by item.
   Fix: remove the late criterion and score the matrix again without it.

3. Every evaluation criterion carries all five parts, a weight, one line of
   rationale for that weight, a direction, and a normalization scheme. The
   weights sum to 100 percent.
   Fix: complete the criterion.

4. Every option carries a pass or fail screening verdict with its reason,
   including the options that were dropped. Where the gate killed every
   option, the record of each failed screen stands in place of the surviving
   set.
   Fix: write the verdict.

5. No option that failed a mandatory criterion was carried into the matrix with
   a low score. Each one was dropped, with the criterion it failed recorded.
   Fix: drop it and rebuild the matrix.

6. Where at least one option survived screening: each survivor was measured
   against every criterion's benchmark on its own, and section 7 shows that
   measurement before section 8 compares anything.
   Fix: redo the per-option analysis before you trust the matrix.

7. Where two or more options survived screening: the matrix shows an
   unweighted total and a weighted total for every option.
   Fix: compute the missing total.

8. Where two or more options survived screening: the weight re-run is
   recorded, the different weights used, the totals under them, and whether
   the ranking held.
   Fix: run it and record the answer.

9. Where two or more options survived screening: the option-set re-run is
   recorded, each non-winning option dropped in turn, the matrix recomputed,
   and whether the ranking of the remaining options changed. Any change is
   disclosed in section 11.
   Fix: run it. "No reversal" is a claim only after you have looked.

10. Every major empirical judgment carries three separate things: a likelihood
    band from the ladder printed at the head of section 9, a confidence stated
    in its own sentence, and a falsifier. Read each judgment line and find all
    three. A judgment that is not empirical carries no band, and a band on one
    fails; it takes the substitute form instead, with confidence still in its
    own sentence: the premise written as a premise, the source of that premise
    named, and, in place of a falsifier, what would have to change for the
    judgment to stop holding.
    Fix: finish the judgment or cut it.

11. Every assumption is marked as an assumption and states what follows if it
    is wrong. Critical assumptions are marked as critical.
    Fix: write the consequence line.

12. The method log records both key-assumptions-check runs, the
    evidence-sensitivity output, and keeps two halves apart: escalation methods
    with the trigger that fired, challenge techniques with who named them.
    Fix: split the halves and add the missing output.

13. Where the authorizing party issued no guidance on what counts as acceptable
    or suitable, the handover names that absence, writes down the standard used
    instead, and marks that standard as your own. The affected stakeholders'
    objectives sit beside the screening verdict, never folded into it.
    Fix: name the absence and label the standard.

14. The second-order pass ran on the recommended course: what changes for each
    actor listed in section 1, and which itemized constraints the course
    consumes. A restatement of the actor list with no stated change is a fail.
    Fix: name one concrete change per affected actor.

15. Exactly one course is recommended. Count the recommended courses; the
    answer is one. The losing options, the contrary evidence, and any
    surviving disagreement are handed over, not omitted. The thesis is one
    sentence. Where two or more options survived screening, section 11 states
    the matrix result in a paragraph a reader can follow without opening the
    matrix. Where no option survived screening, the one course is the single
    change that would readmit an option. A sequenced package is one course
    only if it is named as one and its parts are ordered.

16. Each section is in the form its depth tier allows. At the cheap tier,
    sections 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 12 are tables or row sets, not
    paragraphs.
    Fix: convert the section to its table.

17. Every check that stays failed, and every rule outside this list that an
    operator instruction overrode, has its own deviation block in section 12
    with all three fields: what was deviated from, the authorising rule
    quoted, and what the consumer loses. The first field is the check's
    number, or that rule's name. A named but unquoted authoriser fails. A
    check carrying a block reads failed, never passed. This check does not
    apply to itself. A clean handover carries no block, and that passes.
    Fix: write the block, or go back and fix the check.

## A failing judgment line

Check 10 against "We have high confidence the vendor is very likely to miss the
Q3 date." Likelihood and confidence share a sentence and there is no falsifier.
The fix:

- "The vendor misses the Q3 date: very likely, 80 to 95 percent."
- "Confidence is low; the estimate rests on one unaudited status report."
- "This changes if the vendor publishes a dated integration plan before June."
