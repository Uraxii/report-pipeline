# Deviation block

Only two situations bar a fix: an operator instruction overrides the rule, or
two rules collide and no rewriting satisfies both. Merely expensive is not
barred.

Each deviation is its own block in handover section 12, with three required
fields. A block missing a field is an unrecorded fail.

- **Deviated from.** The check's number in the step 9 check list. Where the
  overridden rule is not a check, its name in the words the skill gives it:
  the contract item, the binding clause, or the step.
- **Authorised by, quoted.** The operator's instruction in the operator's own
  words, or both colliding rules quoted with the side you took. A rule named
  but not quoted authorises nothing.
- **What the consumer loses.**

The check's recorded verdict stays failed.
