# When a document is refused

An access failure is a finding, reported against the question, not a silent
hole. Every kind below gets a consulted-log line.

- **The host refuses the client.** A browser gets the page and a script does
  not. Try the same document from another host carrying it: another arm of the
  same organization, a national archive, a library mirror. When a copy stands
  in for the original, record which host served it and what the original did.
  Do not invent a standard for when a copy is good enough.
- **The document is behind a wall.** Paywalled, login-gated, or licensed. Do
  not route around the wall. Cite only what you can legitimately see, mark in
  its row that the text is not stored, and record in item 7 that the claim
  rests on material you could not keep.
- **The document does not exist, or nobody will give it to you.** Say so in
  item 7, name what you asked for and of whom, and say what the answer rests on
  instead.
