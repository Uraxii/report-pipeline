# Deviation block

Write one block per failed check, and one per rule or contract item an
operator instruction overrode. Two failed checks are two blocks, never one
covering both. Three fields, none optional:

- **Which check**, by its number in the four checks. Where the instruction
  overrode a contract item or a rule instead, name that.
- **What authorises the deviation**, quoted. The operator's instruction in the
  operator's own words, or both colliding rules with the side you took. A
  paraphrase authorises nothing.
- **What the stranger reading the handover loses by it.**

A block short a field, or with nothing named in the first, is an unrecorded
fail. A document you were refused is not a deviation: it goes in item 7 with
the rest of the gaps.
